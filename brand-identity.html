Brand Identity Design

A brand kit you can hand to any designer, editor or printer and get the same result.

What you get

We design the logo system, color palette, type and rules, then package them as a kit. This website uses ours.

  • Logo in primary, positive and monogram versions
  • Round and square avatars for every platform
  • Color palette with exact values and usage rules
  • Typography pairings and type scale
  • Brand guidelines document
  • Social, video and stream templates

Deliverables

  • Logo files in PNG with transparent and solid backgrounds
  • Color values as CSS and JSON
  • Font list with free sources
  • Guidelines in one document

Questions

What do I get at the end?

A zip with every logo version, your palette, fonts and a guidelines document. Same structure as our own kit on this site.

Can you redesign an existing logo?

Yes. We can refine what you have or build a new mark. Tell us what you want to keep.

Will the brand work in video?

Yes. We design with motion in mind: logo reveals, colors that grade well and type that stays legible on screen.
